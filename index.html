About Our School

A Legacy of Excellence in Akure, Ondo State

Our Story

A School Built on Solid Foundation

Kridens-Early Childhood Schools is one of the leading Montessori institutions in Akure, Ondo State. Located on Kridens Avenue, Igbatoro, our school has been dedicated to providing exceptional education that blends Montessori philosophy with strong academic rigour and faith-based values.

We comprise two distinct wings: the Early Childhood Montessori School (ECMS) — serving crèche through primary — and Kridens High School, our secondary school. Together, we offer a seamless educational journey from the earliest years through to secondary graduation.

Our campus provides a safe, green, and stimulating environment where children are free to grow, explore, and become the best versions of themselves.

Our Philosophy

The Montessori Approach

The Montessori method is a child-centred approach that honours the natural development of children. At Kridens ECMS, we implement this philosophy with care, creating prepared environments where children lead their own learning.

Our trained educators act as guides, observing and supporting each child's unique developmental journey rather than imposing a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

Child-led, self-paced learning
Hands-on, materials-based exploration
Mixed-age group collaboration
Intrinsic motivation over external rewards
Respect for the whole child
